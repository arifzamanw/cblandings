RULE 27 COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

LMPC Registration Made Simpler

If your business imports, manufactures or packs pre-packaged commodities for sale or distribution in India, Legal Metrology requirements may apply to you.

Under Rule 27 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011, registration is provided for manufacturers, packers and importers of packaged commodities. The Government's Legal Metrology portal provides an online facility for these registrations.

REGULATORY OVERVIEW

What Is LMPC Registration?

LMPC commonly refers to Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) registration.

It relates to compliance requirements applicable to businesses involved in manufacturing, packing or importing certain pre-packaged commodities.

The Legal Metrology framework is intended to promote accuracy and transparency in weights and measures and protect consumers through prescribed requirements for packaged commodities.

The Department of Consumer Affairs identifies registration of manufacturers, packers and importers under Rule 27 as a function of the Legal Metrology Division.

Core Functions of Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities)

  • Standardization of Packaged Units: Ensures net quantity is declared using standardized units of weight, measure, or number.
  • Mandatory Package Declarations: Prescribes clear display of MRP, manufacturing date, importer/manufacturer details, and consumer care details.
  • Rule 27 Registration: Mandatory registration for entities importing, manufacturing, or packing commodities before market distribution.

CLARIFICATION OF TERMS

LMPC Registration vs Legal Metrology License

These terms are sometimes used interchangeably, but they can refer to different regulatory requirements.

Rule 27 Registration

LMPC Registration

Generally refers to registration of applicable manufacturers, packers and importers under Rule 27 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

Instrument Licenses

Legal Metrology Licenses

Other Legal Metrology permissions or licenses apply to specific activities involving weights and measures, such as manufacturing, repairing, dealing in or importing weighing and measuring instruments.

The requirement depends on what your business actually does.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LMPC registration?
LMPC registration refers to the applicable registration of manufacturers, packers and importers of packaged commodities under Rule 27 of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.
Is LMPC registration mandatory for importers?
Importers of applicable pre-packaged commodities may be required to register under the applicable Legal Metrology provisions and comply with prescribed package declarations. The exact applicability depends on the product and circumstances.
Who issues LMPC registration?
LMPC registration is handled through the Legal Metrology regulatory system. The Department of Consumer Affairs' official portal provides the registration facility for manufacturers, packers and importers under Rule 27.
Is LMPC registration required for manufacturers?
Manufacturers of applicable packaged commodities may fall within the Rule 27 registration framework. The official Legal Metrology portal specifically includes manufacturers, packers and importers in its registration facility.
Is LMPC registration required for packers?
Businesses packing applicable commodities should assess their registration and packaging declaration requirements under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules.
What information must be printed on an imported product?
Depending on the applicable provisions, declarations can include the importer/manufacturer/packer details, country of origin, common or generic name, net quantity, MRP, consumer-care information and other prescribed particulars.
Does every packaged product require LMPC registration?
Not necessarily. Applicability depends on the commodity, packaging, intended use and applicable provisions/exemptions. We recommend checking the specific product rather than assuming that every packaged commodity has the same requirements.
Can CertificationsBay guarantee LMPC approval?
No. CertificationsBay provides consultancy and application assistance. Final registration or approval is subject to the applicable Legal Metrology authority and regulatory requirements.
How long does LMPC registration take?
Processing time can vary depending on the application, documents, authority review and whether additional information or clarification is required. We do not promise a fixed approval timeline because processing is subject to the relevant authority.
Can I apply for LMPC registration online?
The Department of Consumer Affairs provides an online system for registration of manufacturers, packers and importers under Rule 27. CertificationsBay can assist you with navigating the applicable online process.
Does LMPC registration cover weighing and measuring instruments?
Not necessarily. Legal Metrology has separate regulatory services for weighing and measuring instruments, including importer registration and model approval. The applicable requirement depends on the activity and product involved.
