CERTIFICATION SCHEMES

Which BIS Approval Does Your Product Need?

BIS requirements depend on the product, applicable Indian Standard, manufacturer and the relevant Quality Control Order or certification scheme. BIS certification is generally voluntary, but the Government makes certification compulsory for specified products.

01

BIS ISI Mark Certification

For products covered under Scheme I, where conformity is assessed against the applicable Indian Standard and a BIS licence is granted for use of the Standard Mark.

02

BIS CRS Registration

For specified electronics and IT products covered under Scheme II / Compulsory Registration Scheme through lab testing and self-declaration.

03

BIS Scheme X

For products covered under the applicable Scheme X certification requirements for specialized industrial equipment and components.

04

BIS Certificate of Conformity

For products covered under Scheme IV and applicable conformity assessment requirements prescribed by regulatory notifications.

05

BIS for Foreign Manufacturers (FMCS)

Assistance for foreign manufacturers seeking applicable BIS certification for products entering the Indian market. BIS's FMCS covers foreign manufacturers for products other than electronics and IT products covered under the Registration Scheme.

MANDATORY COMPLIANCE EVALUATION

Is BIS Mandatory for Your Product?

Not every product requires mandatory BIS certification. BIS states that its certification scheme is generally voluntary, but the Central Government makes certification compulsory for specified products through applicable Quality Control Orders and other notifications.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BIS Registration?
BIS registration/certification refers to the applicable BIS conformity assessment or registration process for products covered by the relevant BIS scheme or compulsory requirement. The exact process depends on the product and applicable scheme.
Is BIS certification mandatory for every product?
No. BIS states that certification is generally voluntary, although certification is compulsory for products covered by applicable government notifications and Quality Control Orders.
What is the difference between BIS Certification and BIS CRS?
BIS Certification under Scheme I generally involves the ISI Mark scheme and assessment of manufacturing and product conformity. BIS CRS / Scheme II is the registration scheme used for specified electronics and IT products and operates through self-declaration of conformity supported by applicable testing requirements.
Do imported products require BIS?
Not necessarily. BIS requirements depend on the specific product and whether it is covered by a compulsory certification requirement. For products covered by applicable Quality Control Orders, the required BIS approval may be mandatory for import and sale in India.
Do foreign manufacturers need BIS?
Foreign manufacturers may need applicable BIS certification to supply certain products to the Indian market. BIS operates the Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) for applicable products.
Is product testing required?
Testing requirements depend on the applicable BIS scheme and product. For Scheme I certification, BIS states that product conformity is established through testing and assessment of the manufacturer's infrastructure, process controls and testing capabilities.
Can CertificationsBay guarantee BIS approval?
No. We provide professional consultancy, documentation, testing coordination and application assistance. Final approval remains subject to BIS assessment and the applicable regulatory requirements.
How much does BIS registration cost?
There is no single fee applicable to every BIS application. Costs can vary depending on the product, applicable scheme, testing requirements, number of products/models, manufacturing location and government/BIS fees.
