About CPCB EPR Compliance

Under India’s Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) regulations, Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) registration with the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) is mandatory for Producers, Importers, and Brand Owners (PIBOs).

EPR COMPLIANCE FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is required to obtain CPCB EPR Registration?

All Producers, Importers, and Brand Owners (PIBOs) introducing plastic packaging, electronic goods, batteries, or tyres into the Indian market.

What happens if an importer doesn't have an EPR certificate?

Indian Customs may place holds on shipment clearances at ports, and CPCB levies environmental compensation fees for non-compliance.

How are EPR recycling credits fulfilled?

CertificationsBay guides PIBOs on connecting with CPCB-registered recyclers to purchase valid recycling certificates for annual return documentation.
