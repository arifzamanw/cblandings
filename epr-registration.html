REGULATORY OVERVIEW

What Is EPR?

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is an environmental compliance framework under which eligible producers and other regulated entities are responsible for meeting specified obligations relating to the waste generated from products or packaging they place on the market.

The exact obligations differ according to the waste stream and applicable regulations.

Administration & Regulatory Authorities: The regulatory framework is administered through the relevant authorities, including the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and, where applicable, State Pollution Control Boards/other designated authorities.

Depending on the category, compliance can involve:

  • ✓ Registration with the applicable regulatory portal
  • ✓ Providing business and product information
  • ✓ Declaring relevant quantities or sales data
  • ✓ Meeting applicable recycling or EPR obligations
  • ✓ Working with eligible recyclers or other approved entities where required
  • ✓ Procuring or transferring EPR certificates where applicable
  • ✓ Filing required returns and maintaining compliance records
TARGET AUDIENCES

Who May Need EPR Registration?

EPR requirements can apply to different types of businesses depending on the applicable waste-management rules. You may need to assess your EPR obligations if you are a:

01

Manufacturer

Manufacturing products or materials covered under an applicable EPR framework.

02

Importer

Importing regulated products, materials, packaging or other items covered by EPR requirements.

03

Brand Owner

Selling products under your own brand where the applicable EPR framework places obligations on the brand owner.

04

Producer

Introducing products covered by an applicable EPR framework into the Indian market.

05

Other Regulated Entity

Certain EPR frameworks also cover recyclers, refurbishers, plastic waste processors and other stakeholders.

Your exact requirement depends on the nature of your business and the applicable regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EPR registration in India?
EPR registration is a regulatory registration applicable to eligible entities under specific waste-management frameworks. The requirement depends on the type of product, waste stream and role of the business.
Is EPR registration mandatory for every business?
No. EPR requirements depend on the applicable regulations and the nature of your business activities. Manufacturers, importers, producers and brand owners may be covered under different EPR frameworks.
Which types of EPR registration does CertificationsBay assist with?
CertificationsBay provides assistance for major EPR categories including Plastic Packaging EPR, E-Waste EPR, Battery EPR, Waste Tyre EPR, and Used Oil EPR.
Can my business require more than one EPR registration?
Yes. A business can potentially have obligations under multiple EPR frameworks depending on the products it manufactures, imports or sells and the packaging associated with those products.
Do importers need EPR registration?
Importers may be subject to EPR requirements depending on the type of products or materials imported and the applicable regulations.
What documents are required for EPR registration?
Requirements vary by EPR category and business type. Depending on the applicable framework, information may include business registration details, PAN, GST details, authorised-person information, product details, quantities/sales information and other supporting documents. Our team can provide a category-specific document checklist after reviewing your business.
How long does EPR registration take?
Processing time can vary depending on the EPR category, completeness of the application, regulatory review and whether additional information or clarification is requested.
