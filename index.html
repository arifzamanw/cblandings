REGULATORY SCOPE

Understanding EPR Compliance in India

Extended Producer Responsibility is a policy approach under Indian environmental regulations that assigns responsibility to producers, importers, brand owners and other specified entities for managing the end-of-life stage of products and packaging introduced into the market.

Different frameworks are governed by specific rules issued by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) and implemented through regulatory portals managed by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs).

EPR compliance generally involves registering on the applicable portal, calculating annual fulfillment targets, maintaining documentation, purchase of EPR credits where applicable, and filing annual returns.

What EPR Compliance Covers

  • Determining applicability based on product and packaging type
  • Entity classification (Producer, Importer, Brand Owner, Manufacturer)
  • Gathering and organizing sales, import and packaging data
  • Preparing application forms and supporting documentation
  • Portal registration submission assistance
  • Responding to regulatory queries or document clarification requests
  • Ongoing guidance on targets, credit requirements and annual returns
APPLICABLE ENTITIES

Who May Need EPR Registration?

EPR applicability depends on your role in the supply chain and the specific regulated material or product.

Manufacturers

Entities manufacturing regulated products, packaging, batteries or commodities in India.

Importers

Businesses importing products, packaged goods, EEE, batteries, tyres or oil into India.

Brand Owners

Entities selling products under their own registered brand name or trademark in India.

Producers

Entities covered under specific producer definitions in the applicable waste rules.

Other Entities

Recyclers, refurbishers or bulk consumers with specific obligations under certain rules.

EPR COMPLIANCE FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EPR registration in India?
EPR registration is a regulatory registration applicable to eligible entities under specific waste-management frameworks. The requirement depends on the type of product, waste stream and role of the business.
Is EPR registration mandatory for every business?
No. EPR requirements depend on the applicable regulations and the nature of your business activities. Manufacturers, importers, producers and brand owners may be covered under different EPR frameworks.
Which types of EPR registration does CertificationsBay assist with?
CertificationsBay provides assistance for major EPR categories including Plastic Packaging EPR, E-Waste EPR, Battery EPR, Waste Tyre EPR, and Used Oil EPR.
Can my business require more than one EPR registration?
Yes. A business can potentially have obligations under multiple EPR frameworks depending on the products it manufactures, imports or sells and the packaging associated with those products.
Do importers need EPR registration?
Importers may be subject to EPR requirements depending on the type of products or materials imported and the applicable regulations.
What documents are required for EPR registration?
Requirements vary by EPR category and business type. Depending on the applicable framework, information may include business registration details, PAN, GST details, authorised-person information, product details, quantities/sales information and other supporting documents. Our team can provide a category-specific document checklist after reviewing your business.
How long does EPR registration take?
Processing time can vary depending on the EPR category, completeness of the application, regulatory review and whether additional information or clarification is requested. We do not promise a fixed approval timeline because the final processing is subject to the relevant authority and regulatory process.
Can CertificationsBay guarantee EPR approval?
No consultant can legitimately guarantee approval by a regulatory authority. CertificationsBay provides professional assistance with eligibility assessment, documentation, application preparation and regulatory follow-up. Final registration or approval remains subject to the applicable authority and its requirements.
Is CertificationsBay a government agency?
No. CertificationsBay is an independent private consultancy providing compliance, licensing and advisory assistance to businesses.
Do I need to visit a government office?
Many EPR processes are handled through online regulatory portals. Our team provides online assistance with the applicable process, although specific requirements may vary depending on the EPR category and authority.
Do you provide ongoing EPR compliance support?
Yes. Depending on the engagement, CertificationsBay can assist with applicable EPR obligations, certificate-related requirements, returns, reporting and other compliance activities.
