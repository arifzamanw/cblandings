COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a foreign citizen hold 100% shares?

Yes. Under India's automatic FDI route, foreign individuals or corporations can hold 100% equity in an Indian Private Limited company without prior government approval.

Do I need to travel to India for incorporation?

No. The entire process is 100% digital and paperless. All document verifications, signatures, and MCA filings are handled online.

What is the timeline for entity registration?

Incorporation typically takes 7 to 12 business days once all required foreign documents are apostilled or notarized.
