DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS

What Documents Do You Need?

The exact requirements depend on your company structure and circumstances. Commonly required documents may include:

  • PAN & identity proof of directors/shareholders
  • Address proof (Aadhaar, Passport, Driving License)
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Registered office address proof
  • Utility bill (Electricity/Gas/Mobile), where applicable
  • Proposed company name
  • Business activity details
COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people are required to start a Private Limited Company?
A Private Limited Company can generally be incorporated by two or more persons, subject to the applicable requirements.
Can one person register a company?
Yes. An One Person Company (OPC) can be formed by one person, subject to applicable requirements.
How long does company registration take?
Processing time can vary depending on document completeness, name approval, MCA scrutiny and whether clarification or resubmission is required. We do not guarantee a fixed approval timeline.
Is GST included with company registration?
GST registration can be applied for where applicable. GST liability depends on the nature and circumstances of your business.
Can CertificationsBay guarantee company registration?
No. We provide professional assistance with documentation and the application process. Final approval is subject to MCA/ROC review and applicable requirements.
